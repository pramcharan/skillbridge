# SkillBridge: Project Overview & Presentation Script

## 1. Executive Summary
**SkillBridge** is a premium, AI-powered freelance marketplace designed to bridge the gap between elite talent and complex projects. Unlike traditional platforms that rely on manual screening, SkillBridge leverages advanced AI to provide instant, semantic proposal scoring, ensuring the best matches are found in seconds, not days.

---

## 2. The Problem
*   **Manual Fatigue:** Clients spend hours reading through generic proposals.
*   **Keyword Matching:** Traditional search misses highly qualified freelancers who use different terminology.
*   **Trust Gap:** Hard to verify the "cultural and technical fit" without deep interviews.

## 3. Our Solution: The AI Bridge
SkillBridge transforms the recruitment lifecycle:
*   **Semantic Scoring:** Our AI doesn't just look for keywords; it understands the intent and experience level in both job descriptions and freelancer profiles.
*   **Predictive Success:** Each proposal is assigned a 0–100 score with a detailed justification, categorized as **High Match (Green)**, **Potential (Amber)**, or **Low Alignment (Red)**.
*   **Integrated Ecosystem:** From real-time threaded chat to structured dispute resolution, the entire project lifecycle happens within a single, secure platform.

---

## 4. Platform Architecture
### Frontend
*   **Aesthetic:** Premium Dark Mode (Glassmorphism & Neon accents).
*   **Tech:** Vanilla JS (ES6+), CSS Grid/Flexbox, SockJS for real-time responsiveness.
*   **UX:** Interactive dashboards for Clients, Freelancers, and Admins.

### Backend
*   **Core:** Spring Boot 3.4.1 (Java 21).
*   **Security:** JWT Stateless Authentication + OAuth2 (Google/GitHub).
*   **Intelligence:** Integrated AI Orchestrator (supporting OpenRouter/GPT-4/StepFun).
*   **Real-time:** WebSocket (STOMP) for low-latency collaboration.

---

## 5. Key Features Portfolio
1.  **AI Proposal Engine:** Automatic scoring and match explanations.
2.  **Community Hub:** Multi-room chat with presence tracking and emoji reactions.
3.  **Project Management:** Milestone tracking, revision management, and secure file sharing.
4.  **Admin Command Center:** Complete platform oversight, dispute adjudication, and health monitoring.
5.  **Smart Notifications:** Context-aware alerts for every critical project event.
